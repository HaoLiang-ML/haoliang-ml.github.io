Hao Liang is a postdoctoral researcher at University of Maryland, College Park, working with Kaiqing Zhang. He received his Ph.D. from the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, under the supervision of Zhu Han and a postdoctoral training at Microsoft Research. His research interests include reinforcement learning, statistical learning, and optimization. He is particularly interested in robust decision-making, risk-sensitive learning, and causal inference for sequential decision-making.
